# Full pattern catalogue

## 1. Significance and legacy

AI inflates how much things matter, especially anything that could be called heritage, culture, community or a "movement".

| Pattern | Fix |
|---|---|
| stands/serves as a testament/reminder to | delete, or "shows" |
| plays a vital/significant/crucial/pivotal/key role | say what it does |
| a pivotal/key moment, a key turning point | say what changed |
| underscores/highlights its importance/significance | delete |
| reflects broader trends, part of a broader movement | delete, or name the trend with evidence |
| symbolizing its ongoing/enduring/lasting ... | delete |
| setting the stage for | "before", or delete |
| marking/shaping the ... | "when", "this" |
| represents/marks a (significant) shift | "became", "then" |
| evolving landscape, ever-changing world | delete |
| leaves an indelible mark, lasting legacy | delete, or a concrete effect |
| deeply rooted | "old", "long-standing" |
| focal point | "center" |
| more than just a [category] | describe the thing directly |
| "Though minor, X is important because..." | pick one: small or important |

Biology and nature topics: AI over-describes ecosystem roles and conservation status even when nothing is known. Leave it out unless sourced.

## 2. Notability and media coverage

- Listing outlets instead of what they said: "has been featured in local, regional and national media", "profiled in", "garnered attention from". Say what the article said, and name it.
- "independent coverage", "several publications" when there are two. Be honest about the number.
- "maintains an active social media presence" → "posts on Instagram", or delete.
- "written by a leading expert" → name them, or delete.

## 3. Superficial analysis with trailing -ing clauses

The sentence states a fact, then adds a vague comment as a participle phrase:

- "..., highlighting its historical significance."
- "..., demonstrating the ongoing relevance."
- "..., emphasizing the need for collaboration."
- "..., reflecting the community's commitment to..."
- "..., underscoring/showcasing/illustrating ..."

Fix: delete the clause. If the point is real, make it its own sentence with the reason or the source.

Related: inanimate subjects doing thinking ("the data underscores", "the event highlighted"). Say who concluded what.

## 4. Promotional language

nestled, in the heart of, boasts (= has), vibrant, rich (heritage, history, tapestry), breathtaking, stunning, must-visit, renowned, groundbreaking, cutting-edge, state-of-the-art, world-class, seamless, effortless, elevate, unlock, unleash, empower, harness, supercharge, transform your, next-level, to the next level, game-changer, revolutionize, reimagine, crafted, bespoke, tailored, curated, passionate about, look no further, trusted partner, innovative solution, your vision, we believe, diverse array, a wide range of, one-stop shop.

Fix: one concrete fact per claim. "Renowned" → "known for [X]". "A diverse array of services" → list the services. "Unlock your potential" → what the person can actually do afterwards.

## 5. Editorializing and didactic voice

- "It's important to note/remember/consider that", "It is worth noting/mentioning", "Notably,", "Interestingly,", "Keep in mind".
- Unrequested warnings, ethics reminders, "consult a professional" boilerplate.
- "It should be noted that this may vary depending on your location/jurisdiction" when nobody asked.

Fix: delete the frame and keep the fact, if there is one.

## 6. False balance and hedging

AI is trained to sound balanced even when the question isn't open.

- "On the one hand... on the other hand..." when the writer has a view. Pick the side and give the reason.
- Piled hedges: "may potentially", "could possibly", "it can be argued that", "in some cases", "to some extent".
- "While X has its advantages, it also has its challenges." Name the actual advantage and the actual problem.

One hedge where the uncertainty is real is fine.

## 7. Endings and summaries

- "In conclusion", "In summary", "Overall,", "Ultimately,", "All in all", "At the end of the day".
- Paragraphs whose last sentence restates the first.
- "Challenges and Future Prospects" formula: "Despite its [strengths], X faces several challenges..." then "With continued [effort], X is poised to..." / "The future of X lies in its ability to adapt...".
- Infinite rephrasing: the same point said three times in slightly different words.

Fix: stop when the point is made. A real conclusion adds something (a decision, a next step, a number).

## 8. Knowledge-gap filler

- "While specific details are limited/scarce/not widely documented, it is likely that..."
- "As of my last knowledge update...", "information may have changed".
- Confident-sounding guesses wrapped around an unknown.

Fix: "I don't know", "records don't say", or leave the point out. Never invent a fact to fill the hole.

## 9. AI vocabulary

Words Wikipedia's editors found heavily over-represented in post-2022 AI text. Several in one paragraph is the signal; if a paragraph has four or more, rewrite at least half.

additionally (esp. at sentence start), align with, boast(s), bolstered, crucial, delve, demystify, emphasizing, enduring, enhance, fostering, garner, highlight (verb), interplay, intricate/intricacies, key (adjective), landscape (abstract), meticulous/meticulously, navigate (abstract), nuanced, pivotal, realm, robust, showcase, tapestry (abstract), testament, underscore (verb), valuable, vibrant, vital.

Also common in assistant prose: comprehensive, multifaceted, holistic, synergy, paradigm, embark, journey (abstract), dive into / deep dive, unpack, leverage (verb), streamline, ever-evolving, fast-paced, in today's [digital] world/age, it's no secret that, plays a role, at its core, in essence, a testament to, mastery/masterclass.

## 10. Copula avoidance

AI avoids plain "is/are" and "has":

serves as, stands as, functions as, acts as, represents, marks, refers to (in openings), features, offers, boasts, maintains, provides (= has).

Fix: "is", "are", "has". "The museum serves as a hub for local artists" → "Local artists meet and exhibit at the museum."

## 11. Sentence shapes

- **Negative parallelism:** "It's not just X, it's Y", "not only X but also Y", "It's not about X. It's about Y.", "This isn't X. This is Y." State Y.
- **Outline of negatives:** "No fluff. No filler. Just results."
- **Rule of three:** triplets of adjectives or short phrases ("fast, simple, and reliable"), and lists that always have exactly three (or five) items. Use the real count.
- **Forced numbering:** "3 key pillars", "5 core strategies" when the content doesn't divide that way.
- **Elegant variation:** cycling synonyms for one referent (constraints → obstacles → limitations; the company → the firm → the organization). Repeat the word.
- **False range:** "from X to Y" where X and Y are not ends of a scale ("from boardrooms to bedrooms").
- **"such as" before a complete list.** If the list is complete, drop "such as".
- **Vague attribution:** "experts say", "studies show", "many believe", "critics argue", "observers note". Name the source or drop the claim.
- **"Whether you're X or Y, ..."** openers.
- **Rhetorical question headings or openers:** "Why does this matter?", "The result?", "The best part?".
- **Colon reveals:** "Here's the thing:", "The secret:", "The bottom line:".
- **Uniform rhythm:** every sentence 15 to 20 words, every paragraph three sentences. Vary it: some short sentences, some long ones.

## 12. Formatting

- Title Case Headings → sentence case.
- Bold scattered over key phrases → bold almost nothing.
- Inline-header bullets ("**Speed:** ...") on every item → prose, or a plain list.
- Bullet points for connected reasoning → paragraphs.
- Headings on short answers → none.
- Emoji before headings or bullets (✅ 🚀 💡 👉 ✨).
- Em dashes used as all-purpose punctuation → comma, colon, period, parentheses. At most one per paragraph, usually zero.
- Curly quotes mixed with straight ones → be consistent; straight in plain text and code.
- Markdown in places that don't render it (email, Viber/Telegram, Word, form fields, plain .txt) → native formatting or none.
- Nonstandard bullets (•, ▪, →) typed as characters.
- A "Key takeaways" or "TL;DR" box on something short.

## 13. Chatbot residue

Never in a deliverable:

- Openers: Certainly!, Absolutely!, Of course!, Great question!, Sure! Here's..., I'd be happy to help.
- Closers: I hope this helps!, Let me know if you have any (other) questions, Feel free to reach out, Happy to help further, Happy coding!, Good luck with your...!
- Meta lines inside the text: "Here's a polished version:", "Below is a revised draft", "Note: you may want to adjust...".
- "As an AI language model", "I cannot browse", refusals, apologies.
- Unfilled placeholders: [Your Name], [Company Name], [Date], 2025-xx-xx, "Insert statistic here".
- Tool artifacts: contentReference, oaicite, oai_citation, turn0search0, [web:1], grok_card, attached_file, utm_source=chatgpt.com / openai, "↩".
- "Subject:" lines on things that aren't emails.
- Text cut off mid-sentence.

## 14. Openings

- "In today's fast-paced/digital world/age, ..."
- "In the ever-evolving landscape of ..."
- "When it comes to X, ..."
- "Have you ever wondered...?"
- "X is a [adjective] [noun] that [does Y]." as the first line of everything.
- "Imagine a world where..."

Fix: start with the most useful sentence: the answer, the news, the request.

## 15. Russian

Machine Russian has its own set, much of it officialese (канцелярит) plus translated English patterns.

| Pattern | Fix |
|---|---|
| является, представляет собой | это, тире, or rephrase |
| играет ключевую/важную/решающую роль | что именно делает |
| важно отметить, стоит отметить, следует подчеркнуть, необходимо учитывать | delete the frame |
| в современном мире, в эпоху цифровых технологий, в наше время | delete |
| давайте погрузимся / разберёмся / рассмотрим подробнее | just start |
| не просто X, а Y; не только X, но и Y | say Y |
| уникальный, инновационный, широкий спектр, ряд, комплексный, эффективный | the fact itself |
| данный, вышеуказанный, в рамках, на сегодняшний день, осуществлять, производить (действие) | этот, в, сейчас, делать |
| в заключение, подводя итог, таким образом, в целом | delete |
| отглагольные цепочки: «осуществление проверки выполнения заданий» | глагол: «проверю задания» |
| Надеюсь, это поможет! Если остались вопросы, обращайтесь! | delete, or a real next step |
| «Уважаемый(ая) [Имя]» in a messenger | «Добрый вечер, [Имя]!» or just the name |

Messages to students and parents: like a tutor typing on the phone. Greeting, the point in one to three short sentences, the next step. Contractions of speech are fine ("в четверг тогда", "скину в телеграм"). Masculine forms when writing as Koryun.

## 16. Armenian

Use the hy-writer skill: it covers calques (հանդիսանում է, իրականացնում ենք, «X-ի կողմից» passive, «որը» chains), fog words (կատարյալ, անմոռանալի, յուրահատուկ, Բացահայտեք, Վայելեք), spelling, punctuation and the messenger register, and it ships a checker script. This skill's content rules (facts over adjectives, no inflated significance, no chatbot residue) still apply on top.

## 17. Not signs: don't overcorrect

The guide lists these as ineffective indicators:

- Plain, "bland" or "robotic" prose. Clear and dry is fine.
- Fancy, academic or unusual vocabulary, when it's the right word.
- Letter-like formatting in an actual letter.
- Conjunctions (however, moreover, also) in isolation.
- Odd markup caused by editors or browser extensions.

Also: AI detectors flag polished formal writing by humans. A detector score is one signal, not the goal. Don't use "humanizer" services or tricks (random typos, odd synonyms, broken grammar); they make text worse for readers.
